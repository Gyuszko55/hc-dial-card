HC Dial Card

A round thermostat card for Home Assistant, running here in your browser on simulated thermostats – nothing is connected to a real home. Time runs fast here: set a target and watch the room warm up or cool down. The card works with any climate entity; with HC Thermostat it also shows the demand, the decision and the info panel.

Gas boiler, several rooms

HC Thermostat on a gas boiler. The card holds three rooms: the living room, the bedroom with a TRV head and the terrace air conditioner.

Try: long-press the display, turn the ring (or the mouse wheel), long-press again to save · double-tap for the next room · tap the preset icon · tap “i” for the info panel

Air conditioner

An air conditioner that HC Thermostat supervises. The three steps follow the measured power; the pause button hands control back to the AC.

Try: long-press the display, then tap the centre to switch between heating and cooling, or the button at the bottom for on / off · tap the pause icon

Any thermostat

A plain thermostat without HC Thermostat (here a generic thermostat with an electric heater). The card recognises the control type itself.

Try: long-press, turn the ring and just wait – without the second long press nothing is saved and it returns after 15 s · tap for the more-info dialog

Gestures

  • Long press on the display – SET mode
  • Turn the ring (mouse wheel, arrow keys) – change the target
  • Long press again – save; nothing is sent until then
  • Long press on the ring – which room this device follows
  • Double tap – next thermostat on the card
  • Preset icon – preset menu with its end time
  • Button at the bottom – on / off

The first card in YAML

type: custom:hc-dial-card
entity: climate.living_room
weather: weather.home
outdoor: sensor.outdoor_temperature
humidity: sensor.outdoor_humidity
heating: binary_sensor.boiler_burning
decision: sensor.living_room_decision
label: area
rooms:
  Bedroom: { entity: climate.bedroom_trv, trv: true }
  Terrace:
    entity: climate.terrace_ac
    power: sensor.terrace_ac_power

Install

  • HACS → search for HC Dial Card (or add Gyuszko55/hc-dial-card as a custom repository)
  • Add the card from the dashboard editor – it has a visual editor
